# Design Direction: Wufy Portfolio with Anime Japanese Aesthetic

## Identity & Mood
- **Context:** Developer & technology specialist personal portfolio.
- **Visual Mood:** Modern sci-fi tech personal portfolio set against an illustrated anime-space cosmos (`space-bg.webp`), accented with soft cosmic stardust, diamond star twinkles, and occasional shooting stars.
- **Dials:** ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2
- **Motion Purpose:** Ambient cosmic stardust and gentle shooting stars that bring life and wonder to the illustrated outer space background, without distracting from content readability or interaction.

## Color System
- **Base Neutral:** Deep night slate (`#020617`, `slate-900`, `slate-950`).
- **Primary Accent:** Sky Blue (`#0ea5e9` / `sky-500`).
- **Cosmic Accents:** Crystal White, Sky-100, Sky-200, Sky-300, Sky-400.

## Craftsmanship & Accessibility
- Rendered with lightweight, high-performance HTML5 Canvas with zero input latency.
- Completely non-blocking: `pointer-events: none` on the animation canvas (`z-[1]`).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: disables animations for users preferring reduced motion.
- Gentle zero-gravity drift, soft starlight pulsing, subtle interactive cursor wave, and periodic shooting star trajectories.
